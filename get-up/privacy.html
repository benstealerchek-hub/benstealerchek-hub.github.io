Privacy Policy

Get up! · Last updated: 24 October 2026

In short: the app does not collect your data and does not send it anywhere.

What the app stores

Everything you enter stays on your phone:

  • Settings: language, appearance, the figure in the drawings, the hand gripper
  • Your reminder schedule: working days and hours, how often to take a stretch break, meetings
  • Statistics: when you took your stretch breaks, and how many

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. The app has no server, no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not need the internet to work.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications: your phone schedules and shows them itself. The only permission the app asks for is to send notifications, and you can turn them off at any time in your phone’s settings.

Backups and app stores

Your phone may include the app’s data in its backups (iCloud or Google), under Apple’s or Google’s terms; the developer has no access to them. If you have allowed it in your phone’s settings, the App Store and Google Play may share anonymous install statistics and crash reports with the developer. They are used only to fix problems.

Deleting your data

Delete the app, and all of its data is deleted with it.

Children

The app collects no data about anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: benstealerchek@gmail.com
